Why "pending" is a state, not a failure

A gauge that reads 62% on day one of a term is a lie with good typography. When the attendance denominator is not on record, or there is no history to calibrate against, the model returns PENDING with the reason — and the UI shows the reason. The first honest version of this product must be able to say "I cannot tell you yet"; the moment that costs nothing to say, every number on this page means something.
