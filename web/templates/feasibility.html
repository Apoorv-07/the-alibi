A proof,
not a feeling.

CP-SAT answers a question no LLM can: does any schedule exist that satisfies every deadline, eligibility floor, release dependency and sleep cap you are under? When the answer is no, it is a proof — and the unsat core says which facts are jointly impossible.

The unsat core is found by deletion, not by description.

The solver removed one constraint at a time and re-ran; what remains is the smallest set that is still impossible. Removing any single one makes the plan feasible again — that is why the remedy list is short instead of speculative.
