Why the graph is the product. A flat list of "things the assistant knows" cannot be audited: when one entry is wrong you cannot tell whether it was extracted, inferred, or pasted from a rumour, and you cannot tell what else was planned against it. Here supersedes only exists if a higher-authority source contradicted a lower one, and a weaker source that tried to overwrite an authoritative one becomes a conflict, not a correction.
