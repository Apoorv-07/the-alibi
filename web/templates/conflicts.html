The rule, in one sentence

Supersession = recency within an authority tier; across tiers only a source at or above supersede_trust_floor may retire another, and a weaker source that tries to overwrite a stronger one escalates to a review item instead of updating the plan. Recency alone would let last night's rumour beat a signed circular — which is the single most dangerous "smart" behaviour an academic assistant can have.
